# m3.4b.production.v1 manifest and execution contract

`proposed_manifest.json` is planning-only,164 complete configurations. `execution_manifest.json` binds twelve selected entries, generation ID, source identity, authorized maximum16, unchanged resource gates and no proposal authorization. `manifest_binding.json` binds canonical manifest SHA256, byte hashes of every specification and proposed manifest/split/diversity audit. Mutable progress and append-only worker attempt index are separate.

Each entry records native problem ID, specification path/canonical hash, related family, partition, OOD axis and complete descriptor. Numerical body remains native `m3.3b.problem.v1`: coordinates/element connectivity/order, nodal forces/supports, material/SIMP, physical domain/spacing/filter, exact initial array and digest, explicit optimizer version/controls and seed. Mechanical constructor and native loader verify redundant serialization, rigid-mode restraint, unconstrained loads, initial feasibility and deterministic identity. No force snapping, field resizing or optimizer override is introduced. Alternative oblique direction and global sign are explicit force specifications, not new equations.

Families canonicalize left/right mirrors and global force sign; force magnitude/start/volume/seeds and fixed-domain resolution or related domain-growth controls remain together. Aspect and normalized load position and direction families distinguish declared mechanics. Family grouping is conservative, not a proof of independent draws. Continuous-neighbor audit requires matching categorical support and measures normalized load-location, force-line angle/pi and log-aspect distance; threshold.30 produces review candidates without silently merging distinct physics. Every candidate and cross-partition status is retained.

There are18 training families covering aspects1.5/2,three support classes,three directions,four volumes and selected96. Validation and ID test each contain three declared families with grouped48/96 pairs. OOD families cover stable dual-pin support,extrapolated load location,alternate oblique direction,aspect3 and held64 mesh/spacing. Held64 families and their coarse controls stay OOD together; same-physical-problem refinement relatives cannot be split across train/test. Because96 occurs in train,held96 is seen-resolution/unseen-family evaluation,not true unseen-resolution extrapolation.192 is future planning only.

Quotas preserve four volumes per family plus at most two controlled variants: maximum6 vs previous20. Full before/after config and independent-declared-family category counts distinguish family balance from generation count and window balance. Unchanged M3.4 family-first/trajectory/window/stage samplers and bounded native loaders may consume new entries after numerical generation. Only train addresses participate; no held-out/future labels contribute normalization or spatial inputs.

An authorization ledger is persistent across commands and new generations. A batch's IDs/source/generation are reserved together under an exclusive lock; no caller flag changes maximum16. Native workers independently verify this ledger and immutable bindings. Completed IDs remain counted. Source/package mismatch, disk insufficiency, unsupported dimensions and resource/numerical failure fail closed.

Trace summaries reference checksum-protected JSONL and unique attempt IDs. A logical job is its problem ID plus task; each dispatch has a timestamped attempt. Complete and partial attempts remain auditable. Native committed states are journaled independently of monitor progress. A result exists before parent progress publication, so redispatch can integrity-check and reuse it. This is not exactly-once uncommitted FEM execution.
